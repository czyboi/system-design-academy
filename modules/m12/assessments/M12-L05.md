# M12-L05 assessment — Deck and hand composition

## Knowledge checks

**Q1 (LO1).** How many distinct identities must a 4-suit/13-rank deck have, and what are the invariants after 51 deals?

**Answer.** 52 total. The 51 dealt identities and one remaining identity partition the original unique 52; `next_index=51` and no duplicate card appears.

**Q2 (LO1).** What happens on the 52nd and 53rd `deal()` calls in a 52-card round?

**Answer.** The 52nd returns the last unique card and moves next_index to52. The 53rd reports exhaustion and makes no state change; it cannot reuse card0.

**Q3 (LO1).** Why inject RNG into a shuffle and reject mid-round shuffle for this contract?

**Answer.** RNG injection enables repeatable algorithm tests; rejection keeps already-dealt prefix and remaining-deck identity consistent. Repeatable seed does not establish secure/unpredictable randomness.

**Q4 (LO2).** Compute best legal blackjack totals for A,A,K and A,9,A.

**Answer.** A,A,K counts 1+1+10=12; any upgrade busts. A,9,A counts 1+9+1=11, one ace upgraded yields 21.

**Q5 (LO2).** Why cannot Card(ACE) always return 11 as its fixed value?

**Answer.** Its legal value depends on other hand cards. Store immutable rank identity in Card and interpret via a game-specific Scorer over the whole Hand.

**Q6 (LO2).** For K,Q,2, should bestScore silently cap to 21?

**Answer.** No. Every possible total is 22, a bust; the result must communicate bust and raw minimum 22, not a misleading legal score.

## EX-1 — Reusable deck with blackjack scoring

**Scenario.** Implement a 52-card four-suit generic deck with injected RNG, deal exhaustion, immutable Card, Hand and BlackjackScorer. A previous developer put numeric card values on each Card and lets `deal` wrap its index after 52. The test suite must diagnose these defects. A hand receives A,A,K; another A,9,A; a third K,Q,2. The deck deals its final card during a concurrent call and another request arrives immediately afterward.

**Task.** Draw classes and ownership; define card identity/invariants and deal/shuffle/reset contracts; trace final card and exhaustion; compute three hand scores, compare enumerated ace totals with an optimized implementation, and state how to test uniqueness, reproducible shuffle, failed dealing and concurrent calls.

### Reference solution

Card is immutable suit/rank; Deck holds unique 52-card permutation and next_index; a successful deal transfers one card to Hand under a consistent round-owner boundary. Index 51 returns last, sets52, next call exhausts without mutation. A,A,K yields12, A,9,A yields21, K,Q,2 yields22/bust. BlackjackScorer counts each ace as1 and upgrades one by10 only when total stays<=21; the generic deck does not score. Use an injected unbiased-uniform-index RNG interface for shuffle; deterministic test RNG proves repeatability but not production unpredictability. Lock or otherwise serialize dealing; verify no duplicate card under concurrency, 52 unique deals, no 53rd success, no mid-deal shuffle unless contract explicitly allows it, and exhaustive enumeration agrees with optimized ace values.
