# Evidence for M01 integrated design

Checked 2026-11-12. The [approved synthesis outline](../../../curriculum/revision-1/CURRICULUM_MASTER.md#m01-integrated-package-plan) assigns one exercise and outcome M01-S1. The exercise's service, workload, costs, traces and fault probabilities are original model inputs.

| Claim | Source and scope | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Forecast capacity from demand and validate it at the required service level. The exercise's request, byte and work-unit estimates are derived from explicitly chosen parameters. | Google, *Site Reliability Engineering* (2016), [Demand Forecasting and Capacity Planning](https://sre.google/sre-book/introduction/#demand-forecasting-and-capacity-planning). See also [M01-L01 evidence](../evidence/M01-L01.md) and [M01-L02 evidence](../evidence/M01-L02.md). | Verified guidance and modeled arithmetic |
| A current read needs authority and state appropriate for a linearizable read. A reachable replica can serve a weaker read during partition if it can prove the advertised freshness bound. A minority cannot unilaterally claim write authority. | Gilbert and Lynch (2002), [§§2.1–2.3 and §3.1](https://groups.csail.mit.edu/tds/papers/Gilbert/Brewer6.pdf#page=3). Ongaro and Ousterhout, Raft extended paper (2014), [§§5.2–5.4 and §8](https://raft.github.io/raft.pdf#page=13), for majority, ordered log and read authority conditions. The architecture stipulates these properties. A known leader address alone gives insufficient read authority. | Verified principles |
| The original freshness model pairs a local elapsed-time upper bound with an authority barrier applied through a contiguous committed prefix. Every acknowledged write before the local challenge precedes the barrier. At the response decision, the snapshot therefore covers writes acknowledged before the challenge, including any that have since been superseded. | This is a derivation under the brief's ordered-log, challenge-correlation and timer assumptions. Google, *The Site Reliability Workbook* (2018), [Pipeline freshness, coverage, and correctness](https://sre.google/workbook/implementing-slos/#a-worked-example) illustrates the separate idea of verifying freshness with watermarks. The particular protocol here is a model, with no production implementation or benchmark claim. | Derived under stated assumptions |
| A shared edge can bound availability, even with parallel serving regions. Either region being up is useful only if routing, sufficient surviving capacity and the operation's data contract hold. | AWS, *Availability and Beyond*, [Availability with dependencies](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/whitepapers/latest/availability-and-beyond-improving-resilience/availability-with-dependencies.html) and [Availability with redundancy](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/whitepapers/latest/availability-and-beyond-improving-resilience/availability-with-redundancy.html), Eqs. 4 and 5. The exercise's conditional independence is illustrative. | Verified principles and modeled probabilities |
