# Pedagogical review — curriculum plan

**Verdict:** `revise`  
**Artifact:** revision-2 course plan  
**Reviewer:** independent `pedagogy_reviewer`  
**Scope:** sequence, prerequisites, middle/senior audience, outline scope, and teaching-only boundary. No lesson or assessment was reviewed.

## Material finding

| Severity | Location | Finding and learner impact | Recommended change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Medium | SD-07-03, “Web crawling and search indexing” | One outline combines crawler frontier/fetch/politeness/deduplication/recrawl with search parsing/index construction, query retrieval, ranking, and serving. These are distinct subsystems, and the outline does not define their boundary or teaching progression. The scope is too broad to review or author as one independently bounded lesson. | Split crawler ingestion and search/index/query-serving into separate lesson-sized units, or explicitly bound one subsystem and map the other prompt/topic elsewhere. |

## Low-risk suggestion

The optional OOD track is clearly labeled and excluded from the core prerequisite chain. Moving Module 10 to the end, after SD-11-01, would make its appendix role clearer, but its current placement is not a prerequisite blocker.

## Review summary

The broader sequence and prerequisite progression are appropriate for backend-experienced middle/senior learners. No elementary filler issue was identified. The explicit exclusion of quizzes, exercises, and answer content matches the teaching-only scope. The SD-07-03 boundary issue remains material and must be resolved before the plan is finalized.
