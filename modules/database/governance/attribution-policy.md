## Required practices

1. Identify whether a lesson only uses unprotected ideas/facts/methods or adapts protected expression (specific wording, diagrams, selection/arrangement, code, etc.).
2. When adapting Primer expression, include attribution to Donne Martin, the title, original URL, CC BY 4.0 link, and a concise change notice.
3. Do not imply Donne Martin endorses, sponsors or certifies this course.
4. Do not apply additional legal/technical restrictions to the adapted CC BY material that prevent downstream users from exercising CC BY rights.
5. Check each diagram/image/notebook/flashcard and each external linked source separately before redistributing it.
6. Prefer original explanations, diagrams, exercises and code, while preserving traceability to ideas and source materials.
