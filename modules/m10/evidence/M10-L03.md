## Provenance and evidence gaps

### Round 1 evidence clarification — 2026-11-27

[RFC 3986 §3.5](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc3986.html#section-3.5) and [RFC 9110 §7.1](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9110.html#section-7.1), independently opened by the technical reviewer, distinguish fragment identity from the HTTP target. The fetch key excludes the fragment; raw discovery or a separately modeled client-render identity may retain it without claiming a distinct HTTP retrieval.

The frontier cardinality/record size, unique live-document count/postings size, successful-fetch versus attempt/retry populations, burst envelope and two evaluated SLO cohorts are explicit synthetic design inputs. [NIST binary prefixes](https://physics.nist.gov/cuu/Units/binary.html), independently checked in review, supports the units: 2,000,000 × 50 KiB × 30 = 3,072,000,000,000 bytes, about 2.794 TiB before replicas and overhead. Raw retained fetch versions are not automatically distinct indexed documents; source-cut age and client response latency are different quantities. No bandwidth or capacity result is a measured crawler benchmark. Rediscovery-priority merging and generation handling are original frontier policies, not promises of the cited protocols.

The two SLOs use the [SRE specification-to-implementation method](https://sre.google/workbook/implementing-slos/#moving-from-sli-specification-to-sli-implementation) as measurement context. The five-minute query cohort/800 ms client deadline/source-cut age, hourly persisted-change cohort/five-minute all-reader visibility deadline, 99% objectives and 970/1,000 and 960/1,000 outcomes are independent synthetic contracts. They do not measure unfetched-web coverage or imply that a successful fetch makes an index reader current.

Original text, diagrams, URLs, data and assessments; no vendor diagram/code copied. Jaccard and cosine definitions are mathematical choices with explicitly named domains. No live crawl, robots parser, DNS-rebinding experiment, conditional HTTP test, search index reordering experiment or source-to-index latency measurement ran.
