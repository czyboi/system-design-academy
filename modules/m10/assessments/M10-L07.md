# M10-L07 assessment — Category sales ranking

## Explained knowledge checks

**Q1 (LO1).** In the original sample, s1 pA +5, s3 pA +2, s2 pB +3, s5 pB +2 and s4 pC +4 arrive, with a replay of s2. What are the exact unique product totals before corrections?

**Answer.** pA=7, pB=5, pC=4. The duplicate s2 is the same base line and revision and contributes no new sale. For another base line, +3 revision 1 followed by +2 revision 2 contributes only +2 through a cut containing both; replay of revision 1 cannot add 3 again. Attempt-scope deduplication alone cannot protect against a source record repeated across two input partitions.

**Q2 (LO1).** pB belongs to both Kitchen and Gifts under the selected taxonomy policy. Explain why summing category totals exceeds total unique product sales.

**Answer.** It is deliberately counted into each applicable category. Kitchen=7+5=12, Gifts=5, Stationery=4 sum to 21, while product total is 7+5+4=16. The distinction is a business rule, not duplicate source processing.

**Q3 (LO1).** A mapper has correct totals, but different reducers sort their hash partitions locally and concatenate files. Is the resulting ranking globally sorted?

**Answer.** Not necessarily. A global sort needs range partitioning on the composite sort key and ordered partition concatenation, or a single final reducer for a small completed aggregate. Two independently hashed partitions can interleave scores incorrectly.

**Q4 (LO2).** A legitimate return s6 for pA −3 arrives within the permitted correction window. What do pA, Kitchen and Kitchen's top product become?

**Answer.** pA=4, Kitchen=4+5=9, and pB(5) becomes Kitchen's first product above pA(4). Gifts=5 and Stationery=4 are unchanged. Publish a *higher whole-table revision* with a complete source cut; don't partially update one cached row.

**Q5 (LO2).** An old hourly batch finishes after a correction and writes a result with a smaller source cut. Can “last writer wins” be considered correct?

**Answer.** No. Bind an immutable job manifest to its source cut and taxonomy before work. Admit it atomically only if its taxonomy/policy is currently authorized and its complete source coverage does not regress. With current `(tax7,cut109)`, reject/rebuild `(tax8,cut108)` after tax8 is authorized; also reject `(tax7,cut110)` after that authorization. Neither field is a universal lexicographic ordering; same-manifest retries reuse the same input and result. A cache fill captured at the older revision must not populate a current-revision key.

**Q6 (LO2).** A sale event arrives today but its event-time is exactly the weekly end `2026-11-16T00:00:00Z`. Is it in `[Nov9,Nov16)`? How do you prevent a changed product-to-category policy from reassigning half a batch incorrectly?

**Answer.** No; the upper boundary is excluded. Bind the job to one taxonomy snapshot and documented category-at-sale/current-publication policy, and persist the chosen policy and version in the manifest. Arrival time and product's current categories are not automatically the reporting-time values.

## EX-1 — Ranking table under retries and correction

**Scenario.** Weekly window `[Nov9 00:00 UTC, Nov16 00:00 UTC)`. pA belongs to Kitchen, pB to Kitchen and Gifts, pC to Stationery. Unique lines s1 pA+5, s2 pB+3, s3 pA+2, s4 pC+4, s5 pB+2; s2 is replayed. At 2026-11-15 11:05 UTC a permitted late return s6 pA−3 with event time Nov 15 11:00 UTC is durably admitted under its own base ID. The correction cutoff is Nov 16 02:00 UTC exclusive (two hours after the week ends); later arrivals are retained and escalated to an audited adjustment. Every UTC-hour snapshot from Nov 9 01:00 through the final Nov 16 02:00 cut inclusive includes all eligible source inputs admitted before its cut and the authorized taxonomy, and is due within 15 minutes. Thus s6 is excluded from the 11:00 cut and required in the 12:00 cut due by 12:15. A batch with pre-correction data completes **after** the corrected batch. The read API has a cached Kitchen top-list by category name only. The scheduled publication objective is 100% of scheduled hours meeting both deadline and source/taxonomy coverage. Four successive scheduled publications had 3 timely complete eligible outputs and 1 late output. Separately, in a synthetic five-minute ingress cohort of 1,000 distinct original valid reads, 970 received the correct authorized advertised revision within 300 ms, 10 received wrong/stale results, 8 were late, 7 were rejected and 5 failed or were unknown. Retries keep the first-ingress ID and time; the read target is 99%.

**Task.** Describe filter/dedup stage, mapper output, partition/shuffle/reduce of stage 1 and the composite sort of stage 2. Compute product/category totals and rankings before/after s6. Explain taxonomy and timestamp policy, manifest swap and stale cache-fills. Choose batch, incremental or hybrid and specify signals that justify that choice. Calculate both original-read and scheduled-publication success against their separate targets, and state at least two failure tests. Explain the +3 revision 1 → +2 revision 2 replay and both incomparable taxonomy/source-cut cases.

### Reference solution

1. Fix a complete source cut and the UTC half-open window. Group by stable `(source,order_id,line_id)` and select the highest authoritative revision through the cut or a tombstone, then emit at most one current signed value. A replayed +3 rev1 and later +2 rev2 contribute +2, not 5; a return has its own base ID. Map s1 to `Kitchen,pA,+5`, s3 to `Kitchen,pA,+2`, s2 and s5 each to both `Kitchen,pB` and `Gifts,pB`, s4 to `Stationery,pC`. Replay s2 is excluded by stable source identity.
2. Stage-1 reducers sum `Kitchen:pA=7`, `Kitchen:pB=5`, `Gifts:pB=5`, `Stationery:pC=4`. Stage-2 sorts within category by `(-units,product_id)`; category totals Kitchen12, Gifts5, Stationery4. Duplicate category membership explains total 21 vs unique 16.
3. After s6, pA=4; Kitchen total 9, with pB5 then pA4. Publish a new *complete* version, index by `(week,taxonomy_version,source_cut,ranking_revision)`, and atomically redirect serving manifest only if a preassigned immutable manifest matches current authorized taxonomy/policy and its complete coverage does not regress. Reject/rebuild `(tax8,cut108)` against published `(tax7,cut109)` after tax8 authorization, and reject `(tax7,cut110)` under tax8; retries use the same input-bound manifest. No completion-time version ordering can replace these checks.
4. Cache keys and fills capture manifest revision; old Kitchen cache entry cannot masquerade as new version. For “hourly” event processing, run scheduled reproducible batch until measured source volume/late correction requires incremental plus reconciliation. For a hot Job-1 `(week,category,product)` key, salt while retaining the full product key and recombine by that original key. For Job-2 category ordering, merge exact disjoint local top-K using one shared `(-units, product_id)` order, or use ordered ranges for a full result. Category-only reduction loses product ranks.
5. Read SLI = 970/1,000 = 97% for the five-minute original-read cohort, below the 99% target for client receipt of the correct authorized advertised revision within 300 ms. Wrong/stale, late, rejected and failed/unknown count as failures; admitted-only latency is separate. Scheduled publications: 3/4 = 75%, below the 100% target; s6 must appear in the 12:00 cut published by 12:15, and an old fast manifest is insufficient. Tests include s2 replay across logical input splits, upper timestamp boundary, changed taxonomy mid-batch and delayed old job after corrected manifest publication.
