# M10-L07 assessment — Category sales ranking

## Explained knowledge checks

**Q1 (LO1).** In the original sample, s1 pA +5, s3 pA +2, s2 pB +3, s5 pB +2 and s4 pC +4 arrive, with a replay of s2. What are the exact unique product totals before corrections?

**Answer.** pA=7, pB=5, pC=4. The duplicate s2 is the same logical source line and contributes no new sale. Attempt-scope deduplication alone cannot protect against a source record repeated across two input partitions.

**Q2 (LO1).** pB belongs to both Kitchen and Gifts under the selected taxonomy policy. Explain why summing category totals exceeds total unique product sales.

**Answer.** It is deliberately counted into each applicable category. Kitchen=7+5=12, Gifts=5, Stationery=4 sum to 21, while product total is 7+5+4=16. The distinction is a business rule, not duplicate source processing.

**Q3 (LO1).** A mapper has correct totals, but different reducers sort their hash partitions locally and concatenate files. Is the resulting ranking globally sorted?

**Answer.** Not necessarily. A global sort needs range partitioning on the composite sort key and ordered partition concatenation, or a single final reducer for a small completed aggregate. Two independently hashed partitions can interleave scores incorrectly.

**Q4 (LO2).** A legitimate return s6 for pA −3 arrives within the permitted correction window. What do pA, Kitchen and Kitchen's top product become?

**Answer.** pA=4, Kitchen=4+5=9, and pB(5) becomes Kitchen's first product above pA(4). Gifts=5 and Stationery=4 are unchanged. Publish a *higher whole-table revision* with a complete source cut; don't partially update one cached row.

**Q5 (LO2).** An old hourly batch finishes after a correction and writes a result with a smaller source cut. Can “last writer wins” be considered correct?

**Answer.** No. The serving manifest must compare result revision/source-cut and taxonomy version and reject a stale publication. A cache fill captured at the older revision must not populate a current-revision key.

**Q6 (LO2).** A sale event arrives today but its event-time is exactly the weekly end `2026-11-16T00:00:00Z`. Is it in `[Nov9,Nov16)`? How do you prevent a changed product-to-category policy from reassigning half a batch incorrectly?

**Answer.** No; the upper boundary is excluded. Bind the job to one taxonomy snapshot and documented category-at-sale/current-publication policy, and persist the chosen policy and version in the manifest. Arrival time and product's current categories are not automatically the reporting-time values.

## EX-1 — Ranking table under retries and correction

**Scenario.** Weekly window `[Nov9 00:00 UTC, Nov16 00:00 UTC)`. pA belongs to Kitchen, pB to Kitchen and Gifts, pC to Stationery. Unique lines s1 pA+5, s2 pB+3, s3 pA+2, s4 pC+4, s5 pB+2; s2 is replayed. At 11:05 a permitted late return s6 pA−3 with event time within the window arrives. A batch with pre-correction data completes **after** the corrected batch. The read API has a cached Kitchen top-list by category name only. The SLA is hourly refresh and correct current published version, not “any list”.

**Task.** Describe filter/dedup stage, mapper output, partition/shuffle/reduce of stage 1 and the composite sort of stage 2. Compute product/category totals and rankings before/after s6. Explain taxonomy and timestamp policy, manifest swap and stale cache-fills. Choose batch, incremental or hybrid and specify signals that justify that choice. Define original-read success metric and at least two failure tests.

### Reference solution

1. Fix source snapshot, unique canonical line revisions and UTC half-open window. Map s1 to `Kitchen,pA,+5`, s3 to `Kitchen,pA,+2`, s2 and s5 each to both `Kitchen,pB` and `Gifts,pB`, s4 to `Stationery,pC`. Replay s2 is excluded by stable source identity.
2. Stage-1 reducers sum `Kitchen:pA=7`, `Kitchen:pB=5`, `Gifts:pB=5`, `Stationery:pC=4`. Stage-2 sorts within category by `(-units,product_id)`; category totals Kitchen12, Gifts5, Stationery4. Duplicate category membership explains total 21 vs unique 16.
3. After s6, pA=4; Kitchen total 9, with pB5 then pA4. Publish a new *complete* version, index by `(week,taxonomy_version,source_cut,ranking_revision)`, and atomically redirect serving manifest only if the incoming coverage/version supersedes current. An older late job must not change the visible pointer.
4. Cache keys and fills capture manifest revision; old Kitchen cache entry cannot masquerade as new version. For “hourly” event processing, run scheduled reproducible batch until measured source volume/late correction requires incremental plus reconciliation. Salt only measured hot categories with a final exact merge.
5. Rank-reader SLI = original valid API reads receiving current advertised correct version within deadline / all original valid API reads in the ingress cohort. Count wrong, late and failures. Tests include s2 replay across logical input splits, upper timestamp boundary, changed taxonomy mid-batch and delayed old job after corrected manifest publication.
