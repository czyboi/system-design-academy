## Sequence and synthesis

M00-L01 diagnoses a prerequisite gap and teaches a way to select practice. M00-L02 then applies a scoped requirements-to-design method to link sharing, including revocation and host failure. The integrated document-sharing scenario transfers that method to named users, a constrained deployment and a changed failure requirement. The decision summary connects prerequisite repair to L01 and design framing and measured bottleneck decisions to L02. The order and exercise draw on the declared prerequisites without assuming later modules have been taught.

The brief asks the learner to prioritize actors and use cases, name constraints and unknowns, trace publish, download and revoke operations, compare two alternatives and identify evidence that would change the choice. The reference covers both baseline alternatives and a host-loss variant, and the four-criterion rubric accepts another justified design. The host-loss case defines a five-minute observation window, all original authorized requests offered as the population, a full-body deadline and a timely completion threshold. The solution counts unsuccessful requests against the offered population, separates survivor capacity and completed-download latency, and tests revoked-link requests separately. This assessment measures M00-S1 and uses both L02 outcomes. L01 readiness remains in its companion, as the approved synthesis scope requires.
