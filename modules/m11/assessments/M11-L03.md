# M11-L03 companion — Recommendation and graph search

## Six explained checks

**Q1 (LO1).** Candidate retrieval returned p2, p3, p4, p5. Scores are 0.74, 0.81, 0.24, 0.92 respectively; p3 is out of stock, p5 is private. What may the service return?

**Answer.** p2 then p4 under the example's public/in-stock eligibility policy. p3/p5 are excluded **before** user-visible ranking regardless of score; a high relevance score is not authorization or product availability.

**Q2 (LO1).** How is candidate retrieval different from ranking? Why not scan 10 million items on every request by default?

**Answer.** Retrieval narrows the entire eligible corpus to a bounded candidate set using an index/model; ranking uses richer features on that shortlist. Full scoring can have large online CPU/storage/latency cost, but retrieval has its own recall/coverage trade-off. The chosen index/source cut must be measured.

**Q3 (LO1).** An event processor replays the same click. What key and measurement ensure it does not inflate interest? Does a click alone prove the item was displayed?

**Answer.** Deduplicate by a stable original event ID within a declared source scope and track impressions separately. A click log without displayed/impression context can distort training and evaluation. Feedback should be consented, scoped, versioned and privacy-controlled.

**Q4 (LO2).** Viewer V can see friendship edges a-b, b-c and b-f, but not a-d or d-e. Which Da Nang people within two permitted hops are eligible?

**Answer.** b, c and f. e is globally two hops via a-d-e but that route is hidden; its city and high score do not give a permitted social connection. A location index returns candidates, not an authorization proof.

**Q5 (LO2).** A product's popularity feature is one day old. Another product's private-access bit is stale after an explicit revocation. Are these the same class of failure?

**Answer.** No. Old popularity can reduce relevance and may be tolerated by a declared freshness budget. A stale permission can disclose forbidden content and violates a hard security boundary; a final authoritative check/manifest is needed.

**Q6 (LO2).** The graph query promises every eligible person reachable within two hops. One person server times out. Is returning an unqualified “only b, c” answer correct?

**Answer.** Not necessarily. Missing adjacency may contain other eligible people. Retry within budget or label incomplete/fail under the contract; a ranked partial top-K is different from exhaustive reachability or a shortest-path proof.

## EX-1 — One ranking decision, one graph privacy decision

**Inputs.** Product candidate p2 features (0.8 similarity,0.7 interest,0.5 popularity); p3 (0.9,0.8,0.3) out of stock; p4 (0.2,0.1,0.9); p5 (1,0.8,0.8) private. Toy score `0.6*sim + 0.3*interest + 0.1*popularity`. Graph edges `a-b,a-d,b-c,d-e,b-f`; viewer sees only `a-b,b-c,b-f`. b,c,e,f are in Da Nang; city index is eventually updated, and a rights service can change instantly.

**Deliverables.** Produce data/event flow from feedback to retrieval/ranking/response, scores and eligible product order; classify stale model/stock/private features. Draw two-hop graph traversal and a location-index-intersection plan, explain why e is excluded; compare offline precompute, online index retrieval, graph traversal and permission recheck, with a budget and a falsifying race test. Define an original request success SLI distinct from experiment relevance.

### Reference solution

- Candidate scores: p2=0.48+0.21+0.05=0.74, p3=0.54+0.24+0.03=0.81, p4=0.12+0.03+0.09=0.24, p5=0.6+0.24+0.08=0.92. Hard filters remove p3 (stock) and p5 (private). The eligible ranking is p2 then p4; do not disclose excluded scores to a forbidden viewer.
- Feedback events persist with stable IDs and consent; offline features/model versions generate candidate index and an online feature snapshot. Rank only a bounded candidate set then re-check current item rights/stock before output; measurement of displayed impressions supports relevance experiments.
- For viewer V, only a→b and b→c/f are allowed. b/c/f in Da Nang are eligible; e is not accessible via hidden a-d. An index for city=DaNang can reduce candidate volume, but final graph/permission checks are mandatory. If required adjacency is unavailable, a complete claim is unsupported.
- An old popularity feature is a relevance/freshness concern; an old private flag is a confidentiality error. A policy-gated source/cut and permission-change race are testable. Report all original valid queries in the denominator with client receipt deadline and authorization, and a separate relevance metric that cannot be faked by returning nothing.
- Test a rights revocation between candidate retrieval and final serving; cache fill after revocation; high-degree graph node; and a missing partition returning partial candidates.
