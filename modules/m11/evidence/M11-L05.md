# M11-L05 primary evidence

Checked 2026-11-16. 42/8/2 layout, timestamps 5000/4999, worker IDs and SLO values are **original teaching constructs**, not Snowflake/UUID vendor configurations.

| Claim | Primary evidence | Verification and boundaries |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C1. UUIDv4 randomized namespace and UUIDv7 timestamp/counter guidance. | IETF [RFC 9562 §§5.4,5.7,6.2–6.4](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9562.html), May 2024; checked 2026-11-16. | UUIDv4 normally 122 random bits; v7 time based and counter algorithm optional; no global commit-order or security guarantee from UUID format alone. |
| C2. | C3. Blind atomic counter increments are not idempotent across retries. | AWS [DynamoDB: Working with items and attributes, Atomic counters](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/amazondynamodb/latest/developerguide/WorkingWithItems.html), checked 2026-11-16. | Named product example; the range reservation and input-bound receipt are proposed application mechanisms, not automatically provided by counter alone. |
| C4. Coordinator lease ownership and external fencing are distinct. | Academy [M09-L03](../../m09/lessons/M09-L03.md), on unmerged MS05 Draft PR #100, supported by historical Chubby paper referenced in its evidence. | No claim that a lease prevents an isolated old process from producing IDs. |
