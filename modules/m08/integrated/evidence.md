# Evidence for M08-EX1

Verified against the SDA-v1 M08 integration plan. The sales and search datasets, deadlines and drill counts are original scenario inputs. No throughput or product latency is implied.

| Integrated claim | Supporting evidence and applicability |
| --- | --- |
| Map, partition and reduce the snapshot, then range-sort the completed totals. Use attempt-scoped outputs. | [M08-L01 evidence](../evidence/M08-L01.md), C1–C3. Original MapReduce, §§2–4 and §5.3. The reducers, salt proposal and totals belong to this exercise. |
| Event-time membership, late corrections and deduplication depend on window and sink policy. | [M08-L02 evidence](../evidence/M08-L02.md), C1–C3. The archive cutoff and version contract are assumptions, not an engine's default. |
| Disjoint shards and comparable scores permit a top-k merge. A timeout can require an explicit partial result or a failure, while a source cut describes freshness separately. | [M08-L03 evidence](../evidence/M08-L03.md), C1–C4. Cutoff math and the merge order are derived from the original scenario. Manifest freshness is an application-level proof requirement. |
| Live initialization must resume from the position bound to its complete snapshot; publication recovery needs durable terminal proof across later replacements. | [Spark 4.0.1 checkpointing](https://spark.apache.org/docs/4.0.1/streaming/apis-on-dataframes-and-datasets.html#recovering-from-failures-with-checkpointing) and [fault-tolerance semantics](https://spark.apache.org/docs/4.0.1/streaming/getting-started.html#fault-tolerance-semantics) provide state/progress and sink context, independently checked in the technical synthesis review. The exclusive `p6` continuation, atomic recoverable initialization, phase order, stable final-intent receipt and retention rules are explicit application obligations; Spark does not automatically implement this scenario's sink. |
| A freshness manifest needs a complete source-to-log boundary, durable apply and query-visible coverage on every eligible reader; reads keep a fixed generation, shard set and compatible view. | [AWS transactional outbox, Issues and considerations](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/prescriptive-guidance/latest/cloud-design-patterns/transactional-outbox.html#issues-and-considerations), [Elasticsearch 8.19 near-real-time search](https://www.elastic.co/guide/en/elasticsearch/reference/8.19/near-real-time.html) and [Refresh API](https://www.elastic.co/guide/en/elasticsearch/reference/8.19/indices-refresh.html), independently checked in the synthesis review. Relay completeness and reader visibility are distinct. The manifest, immutable `g17`, one-reply-per-logical-shard rule and three-minute freshness threshold are application contracts, not automatic Elasticsearch guarantees. |
| A bounded approximate ranking counter set does not bound all deduplication, active-window or recovery state. | [Metwally et al., Space-Saving, §3 and error analysis](https://www.cs.emory.edu/~cheung/Courses/584/Syllabus/papers/Frequency-count/2005-Metwally-Top-k-elements.pdf), independently checked in the synthesis review, plus accepted M08-L02 evidence C2–C3. Budget event IDs, checkpoints and retained repair history separately from the algorithm's counter set. |
