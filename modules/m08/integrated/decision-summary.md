# Choose processing and search contracts

## Trace the ranking and search decisions

One provisional sales snapshot is an immutable complete intake prefix: persist its exclusive continuation with initialized sums, seen IDs and publication intent so admissions during startup are read, not skipped. It supports a reproducible count. Map five units of Mugs on one split and three on the other to the same grouping key. Shuffle that key to one logical reducer, then put the completed total of eight into the sales-ordering job. Accept one published result per logical unit of work even if a worker retries. Keep the sales event identity through the lateness window so a replayed five units cannot raise the Mugs total to thirteen. The new three-unit `s7` sale raises it to eleven in a provisional revision because its durable first admission precedes the serialized 11:05 close marker. Drain and acknowledge the final live result before publishing any archived correction in this exercise; `s8`, first admitted at 11:07, increases Filters from four to six in a later whole-result correction. The event-time progress estimate never substitutes for the durable admission order. Persist a terminal final intent and assign verified archive cuts stable revisions before replay; retire live state only after confirming a durable receipt keyed to its final intent. The receipt survives later correction replacement and remains until retry obligations end, so a writer recovering after a lost acknowledgement can prove final completion without overwriting a correction. Publish whole revisions to make retries and repair observable to readers. [M08-L01](../lessons/M08-L01.md) and [M08-L02](../lessons/M08-L02.md).

Search has a different wait boundary. Before the deadline, the coordinator can compare only the scores from responding shards using the requested index generation. A missing shard makes the list provisional even when that generation is fresh. A complete list can still be too old or unverified for export. Prove relay publication through the chosen source cut, durable index application and query visibility on every eligible reader; bind the manifest to generation, logical shards and a compatible read view through query, fetch and retries. Accept one reply per logical shard. The proof is an application contract, not an index checkpoint alone. Choose coverage and freshness contracts for the caller, and record a complete, correct response received on time separately from the latency distribution of responses that succeeded. [M08-L03](../lessons/M08-L03.md).
