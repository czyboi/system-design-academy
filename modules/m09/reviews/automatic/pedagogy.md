## Integration, sequence and exercise

The lesson sequence is coherent: L01 establishes record authorization and transport boundaries, L02 uses those boundaries in diagnostics and restore evidence, and L03 applies measured recovery and failure assumptions to stale ownership and coordination. The decision summary introduces the externally visible invariants before the ledger, then connects per-request authorization, queue and worker trust, cohort telemetry, recovery evidence and storage-side fencing. It gives decision criteria and limitations rather than repeating complete lessons. The brief provides the request path, failure assumptions and numbers needed for a learner to apply the prerequisite mechanisms without using the reference answer.

For MS05-M09-SYN-01, the current brief asks explicitly for authorized producer and consumer identities, a server-scoped integrity-protected job, reauthorization at worker execution and result retrieval, authenticated worker-to-database transport, a distinct limited principal, tenant-scoped access and fail-closed revoked/unavailable membership. The decision summary provides the causal connections and the rubric requires these boundaries. This independently supports the *pedagogical and assessment coverage* of the author-side correction; the separate technical reviewer determines whether the mechanisms and cited sources are correct.

The exclusive trace totals 40+50+760+50+80+160+40+20 = 1,200 ms, with 760 ms queue wait; the parent span is not added twice. The cohort categories sum to 1,000, with 930 timely validated results, 20 late, 20 rejected, 20 timed out and ten failed: 93% versus a 99% objective. The prompt and solution use all originals in the five-minute arrival cohort, follow each to its two-second deadline and distinguish admitted-only latency, subject to the rubric requirement in M09-SYN-PED-01. The fence scenario requires confirmed storage installation before W2 writes, acknowledges the pre-install interval and distinguishes election from validated database restoration. The test plan checks original client outcomes during failover and preserves the coordination/data-recovery distinction. These claims are evaluated here for teaching and assessment coherence, not implementation verification.
