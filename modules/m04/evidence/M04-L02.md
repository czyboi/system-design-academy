# Source evidence for M04-L02

Checked 2026-11-13. Each row verifies the stated mechanism at its stated scope. Profile values, log positions and failure traces are original assumptions.

| Claim | Supported statement | Primary evidence and scope | Limits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| REPL-1 | A PostgreSQL standby streams WAL and can serve read-only queries once in hot standby. Asynchronous shipping can leave committed work absent on the candidate at failover. | [PostgreSQL 18 §§26.2.1, 26.2.5](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/warm-standby.html#STREAMING-REPLICATION) | A replica may receive WAL before applying it. Read freshness is endpoint- and snapshot-specific. |
| REPL-2 | With named synchronous standbys, `on` waits for durable remote WAL flush and `remote_apply` waits for replay on the required standbys. | [PostgreSQL 18 §19.5.1](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/runtime-config-wal.html#GUC-SYNCHRONOUS-COMMIT), [§26.2.8](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/warm-standby.html#SYNCHRONOUS-REPLICATION) | Ack policy does not protect promotion of a different stale standby. Loss of the required acknowledgers can delay commit responses. |
| REPL-3 | Promotion needs a mechanism to prevent the old primary from serving as primary after recovery. | [PostgreSQL 18 §26.3](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/warm-standby-failover.html) | Failure detection, fencing and routing are separate control responsibilities. |
| REPL-4 | The original Dynamo paper used causal version metadata to detect concurrent branches and let clients reconcile them. | [DeCandia et al., Dynamo, SOSP 2007 §4.4 and figure 3](https://www.allthingsdistributed.com/files/amazon-dynamo-sosp2007.pdf#page=6) | Causal ordering is the cited mechanism. The profile field merge is an illustrative application policy. This paper does not specify DynamoDB guarantees. |
| REPL-5 | Synchronous acknowledgement adds communication wait, and replay throughput and WAL bandwidth affect the ability to keep a replica current. | [PostgreSQL 18 §§26.2.5, 26.2.8.3](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/18/warm-standby.html) | A replica can offload eligible reads. It still has to apply writes and the leader still executes them. |
