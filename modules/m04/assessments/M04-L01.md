# Companion assessment for M04-L01

## Practical exercise

**EX-1.** An equipment supplier lets business accounts reserve one or more units of a SKU. A confirmed reservation must name an existing account, contain its reservation line and reduce available stock exactly once within the database operation. Support may close accounts, and historical reservations must survive. The portal lists each account's newest 20 reservations. A large PDF packing slip may appear after confirmation. Assume PostgreSQL 18 Read Committed for the sample design.

Model the tables, keys and commit boundary. Trace two requests for the last unit and the outcome if the winner's line insert fails. Describe the history access path and distinguish current account fields from an agreed reservation price. Compare database bytes with an external PDF reference. Trace a worker crash after the upload and explain how the pending reservation becomes ready and how abandoned bytes are retired.

## Knowledge checks

### Q1

Which constraints make the account relationship mandatory and preserve confirmed reservations when support attempts to delete an account?

**Answer.** A non-null account foreign key with restrictive deletion. Support can keep a closed account row. The reference stays valid and historical reservations keep their parent.

### Q2

A claim reduced stock inside the transaction. The insert of the reservation line fails. What can the application acknowledge?

**Answer.** It can report failure after rolling back. The reservation header, line changes and claim share the transaction, so the stock returns to its prior value.

### Q3

How does the database prevent both clients that previously read quantity one from each claiming one?

**Answer.** Both issue a conditional decrement against the same row. The second updater waits and PostgreSQL rechecks the predicate on the committed row. If the first claim commits, the second updates zero rows.

### Q4

Should the reservation copy the live account display name and the agreed unit price?

**Answer.** The live name can be read from the authoritative account row. The agreed price is its own historical fact and belongs on the reservation line when later catalogue changes must leave past totals intact.

### Q5

Which access path would you propose for the newest 20 reservations per account, and what cost would you measure?

**Answer.** An account-leading composite index with descending creation time and a unique tie-breaker supports filtering and ordered retrieval. Compare the chosen read plan and write overhead against a representative workload.

### Q6

Why might a tiny signed record belong in the database while the packing slip uses object storage?

**Answer.** The tiny record can share the reservation's commit when its existence is mandatory at confirmation. The PDF can be generated later and has a separate pending state, which allows independent storage and recovery.

## Sample solution and rubric

Use account, reservation, reservation line and stock tables. Preserve closed accounts and enforce the mandatory relationship through the database. In one transaction conditionally decrement stock and write the reservation and line, aborting on a zero-row claim or any write failure. On successful commit, acknowledge the reservation and show the slip as pending. Find history with `(account_id, created_at DESC, id DESC)` and preserve agreed prices on lines.

Upload each slip attempt to a distinct immutable key. Publish the key with a guarded transition from pending to ready. Retry pending slips after a crash. Reconcile abandoned objects after retiring the attempts that own them, retaining any key still referenced by a ready slip.

| Criterion | Points | Full credit |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Relational model | 2 | Valid account reference, stable keys, deletion policy and transaction boundary agree with the rules. |
| Concurrent trace | 3 | Shows one successful claim at most, names the guard and traces the effect of a rollback on the other attempt. |
| Read and historical data | 2 | Explains the account-leading access path, its write cost and why agreed price is stored separately from current fields. |
| Attachment lifecycle | 3 | Distinguishes the two storage boundaries, shows retry and safe cleanup, and keeps the reservation usable while the PDF is pending. |

Award partial credit for correct mechanisms with incomplete traces. Accept equivalent transactional enforcement and another attachment design that respects the same success and failure rules.
