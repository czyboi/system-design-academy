# Source evidence for M07-INTEGRATION

Checked 2026-11-15 against the SDA-v1 module and scoped lesson evidence. Workload rates, processing budgets and observations are illustrative.

| Design decision | Evidence and scope |
| --- | --- |
| Accept one durable job with a recoverable delivery intent | The source transaction pairs job state with an outbox entry. Repeated publication is possible. The scenario scopes identity to caller and request key and the acceptance outcome to the durable record. Service ownership follows M03-L01. [M07-L01 evidence](../evidence/M07-L01.md), [M03-L01](../../m03/lessons/M03-L01.md), [AWS transactional outbox](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/prescriptive-guidance/latest/cloud-design-patterns/transactional-outbox.html). |
| Handle duplicate delivery and terminal failure | SQS Standard can redeliver. Its receipt handle is per receive and a DLQ receive count concerns messages. The job authority enforces one terminal result and guards publication and cleanup. [M07-L01 evidence](../evidence/M07-L01.md), [SQS Standard delivery](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/AWSSimpleQueueService/latest/SQSDeveloperGuide/standard-queues-at-least-once-delivery.html), [SQS DeleteMessage](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/AWSSimpleQueueService/latest/APIReference/API_DeleteMessage.html). |
| Bound admission and retries to execution capacity and the deadline | Waiting, total nonterminal, start-rate and in-flight limits protect different quantities. Retry pacing redistributes load while the shared execution allowance still applies. [M07-L02 evidence](../evidence/M07-L02.md), [Google SRE, Latency and Deadlines](https://sre.google/sre-book/addressing-cascading-failures/#latency-and-deadlines), [AWS Builders' Library, backoff and jitter](https://aws.amazon.com/builders-library/timeouts-retries-and-backoff-with-jitter/). |
| Choose a retained log for independent readers and replay | The decision summary uses the standard Kafka consumer-group example and retained positions. Log reconstruction belongs to its lesson and companion. [M07-L03 evidence](../evidence/M07-L03.md), [Kafka 4.2, Consumer Position](https://kafka.apache.org/42/design/design/). |

M07-L01 refers to M03-L01 for ownership and M07-L02 for backlog, admission and retry budgets. M07-S1 assesses the duplicate-safe lifecycle and overload choice using M07-L01-LO1 and M07-L02-LO2. Each lesson companion retains its full outcome coverage.
