# Companion assessment for M07-L03

## Practical exercise

**EX-1.** A marketplace index saved a snapshot paired with next positions P0:12 and P1:8. P0:12 is a full update of listing L to revision 4, P0:13 repeats it, P0:14 changes listing M to revision 2 and P0:15 deletes L at revision 5. P1:8 updates listing K to revision 9. The indexer crashes after applying P0:12 but before saving progress. An alerting group is already past these positions. Trace replay and checkpointing, state the cutover condition and explain how you would recover if P0's earliest retained offset moves to 15 before replay starts. Defend a log or queue for index updates and say how one hot listing affects parallelism.

## Knowledge checks

### Q1

Does an alerting group's progress advance the search group's position?

**Answer.** No. Each group tracks its own progress in the retained log.

### Q2

What is the next position after applying contiguous records P0:12 through P0:15?

**Answer.** P0:16. P1 is tracked separately.

### Q3

A delayed replay sends an older full value for a deleted listing. What stops resurrection?

**Answer.** Keep the deletion revision marker, and reject an older revision when applying the replay.

### Q4

Would committing a position before writing the index avoid duplicates safely?

**Answer.** A crash between them can skip the missing effect. Apply durably before advancing and make repeats safe, or commit effect and position together.

### Q5

Can an extra consumer process the busiest listing's ordered partition in parallel without changing the contract?

**Answer.** Another group member can process another assigned partition. The single ordered key still needs serialization or an application-specific coordinated ordering scheme.

### Q6

Can the rebuild always start at the log's earliest retained offset if its saved cursor is gone?

**Answer.** No. Missing records can invalidate the snapshot continuation. Use a newer consistent snapshot or reconcile against the authority with a matching catch-up boundary.

## Sample solution and rubric

Restore the snapshot into a separate generation at P0:12 and P1:8. Repeat P0:12 after the crash. An already-applied version 4 in that generation makes the repeat harmless. Apply the duplicate as a no-op, apply M's change, keep a revision 5 deletion marker for L and apply K independently. Advance to P0:16 and P1:9 only after those effects or no-ops are durable. Preserve a validated, gap-free applied prefix for each partition, and cut over after the replacement covers at least the old served index's frozen applied vector. If the required continuation was deleted, starting at 15 leaves M stale. Take a new snapshot with known continuation positions or reconcile the new index to the authoritative catalogue including removals and overlapping changes. Independent consumers and replay support choosing a retained log here. Compare the retention and hot-key costs with a work queue for one-time, per-item execution.

| Criterion | Points | Full credit |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Replay and failure | 4 | Traces both partitions, duplicate after failed checkpoint, deletion marker and next positions. |
| Repair and cutover | 3 | Detects missing history and pairs new state with its own continuation and verified cutover. |
| Messaging choice | 3 | Explains independent progress, retention and ordering or hot-key cost. |

Accept alternatives that satisfy the same constraints. Award partial credit for a correct trace with an incomplete justification.
