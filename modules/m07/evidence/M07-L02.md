# Source evidence for M07-L02

Checked 2026-11-14. All arrival curves, deadlines and rate limits are illustrative models with stated assumptions.

| Claim | Primary evidence | Applicability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C1. Long-run average number in a stable system relates to effective arrival rate and average residence time. | John D. C. Little, Operations Research 9(3), 1961 [A Proof for the Queuing Formula, theorem and queue-only boundary, pp. 383 and 387](https://fisherp.scripts.mit.edu/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2015/11/ContentServer.pdf#page=5). | Verified for compatible long-run averages and a consistent boundary. A burst trace needs a finite-window flow model. |
| C2. Rejecting at overload and bounding offered traffic can protect capacity. Criticality can guide admission, and client throttling can reduce requests the backend cannot handle. | Google SRE [Handling Overload, Client-Side Throttling, Criticality and Utilization Signals](https://sre.google/sre-book/handling-overload/). | Verified mechanisms from an operator's account. Thresholds and fairness are workload choices. |
| C3. Retrying across layers amplifies work. Capped exponential backoff, jitter and local retry budgets can reduce load and synchronize fewer retry attempts. | AWS Builders' Library [Timeouts, retries, and backoff with jitter](https://aws.amazon.com/builders-library/timeouts-retries-and-backoff-with-jitter/), [Exponential Backoff and Jitter, Adding Jitter](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/architecture/exponential-backoff-and-jitter/#Adding_Jitter), [Google SRE, latency and deadlines](https://sre.google/sre-book/addressing-cascading-failures/#latency-and-deadlines), [AWS safe idempotent retries](https://aws.amazon.com/builders-library/making-retries-safe-with-idempotent-APIs/). | Verified techniques. The full completion-time gate and scenario arithmetic are design choices; idempotent side effects and an end-to-end deadline remain necessary. |
