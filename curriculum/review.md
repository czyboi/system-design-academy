## Pedagogy findings

### P1 — Major, open: integrated assessment promises exceed the proposed exercise

At `CURRICULUM_MASTER.md:968-972`, M10 asks learners to compare two content-platform designs but claims every LO of all eight disparate case studies is assessed. The same correction applies to CP-01's blanket “every outcome” promise: identify the cross-module transfer outcomes it actually measures and rely on the lesson/module assessment portfolio for remaining coverage. No assessment content needs to be authored now.

### P2 — Major, open: two lesson boundaries combine independent design problems without enough instructional room

M11-L05 combines a unique-ID service (random/coordinated/time-ordered IDs, clock rollback and worker identity) with four rate-limit algorithm families and distributed admission policy. M11-L07 combines tracing garbage collection/concurrent reclamation with Redis-like durable store and Memcached-like cache architecture. Each has separate invariants, mechanisms, failure traces and worked problems; the current three-item outline and single generic outcome pair do not define a bounded lesson an author can execute without choosing what to omit.

Give IDs and rate limiting separate lesson IDs and worked examples. Give memory reclamation its own lesson, with durable-store versus disposable-cache composition in a separate lesson or an explicitly owned existing store/cache lesson. Adjust matrix rows and prerequisites. Other paired domains may remain comparisons if the intended limits and distinct failure traces are stated; splitting every comparison is not required.
